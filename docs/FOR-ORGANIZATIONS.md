# For organizations: we built it, you run it, we support it

Walk With Us is free software for running a **safe-passage program**: trained adult volunteers on public corners at school arrival and dismissal, plus "walk with me" requests answered by **two** adults. Your organization runs the program. The software and the technical support are free.

## What your organization provides (the program)

1. **A named lead and at least one coordinator** reachable during your published hours (for example 7:30–8:45 and 2:30–3:45 on school days).
2. **Corners and hours.** You choose the public corners and the times you can actually cover. Publish only what you can staff.
3. **Volunteers you have met.** Every volunteer is met in person, briefed (see `VOLUNTEER-BRIEFING.md`) and approved by you before their first shift. Background checks, if you require them, are run by you with the volunteer's written consent through an FCRA-compliant provider.
4. **Pairing.** Walks go to two adults, ideally from different households. The software enforces two; you choose who.
5. **Insurance and governance.** General liability coverage for volunteer activity, and your own policies. If you are not a 501(c)(3), consider a fiscal sponsor.
6. **Relationships.** Let the school(s) and your local precinct's community affairs office know the program exists. Volunteers do not act as police and do not engage.

## What this is not

- **Not an emergency service.** Danger means 911, every time. The app shows 911, NYPD Crime Stoppers and 988 on every screen.
- **Not a tracking app.** It never asks for or stores anyone's location or any child's name.
- **Not a reporting system** for incidents or people. Tips go to NYPD Crime Stoppers; school matters go through the school (Chancellor's Regulations A-449 and A-832).
- **Not legal advice**, and the maintainers do not supervise volunteers. Your organization runs the program and makes the decisions.

## Hosting choices

- **Hosted for you (simplest).** Your program lives on the shared deployment with its own page, coordinators and data, isolated by organization.
- **Self-hosted (full control).** Deploy the open-source code to your own Cloudflare account in about 30 minutes; we help.

Either way the cost is $0 for typical neighborhood programs.

## Your data

Your organization controls its program's data. Family callback numbers are deleted automatically 72 hours after each walk. Volunteer records stay until you remove them. If you stop running the program, we delete your organization's data on request and confirm in writing.
