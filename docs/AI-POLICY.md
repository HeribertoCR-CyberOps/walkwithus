# AI policy

**Walk With Us contains no AI at runtime, and never will in the safety path.**

| Question | Answer | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Does any family, volunteer or coordinator data go to an AI service (Claude, ChatGPT/ASTRA, Gemini, Workers AI, or any other)? | **No.** | The privacy wall would end the moment a phone number or request left the system. External model APIs are third parties. |
| Does an AI decide anything — who walks, what is urgent, who is approved? | **No.** | Safety decisions must be deterministic, explainable and made by an accountable person. |
| Is there a chatbot for families? | **No.** | It could give wrong safety advice. The page gives one instruction for danger: call 911. |
| Is AI used at all? | **Only at build time, on non-personal material.** | Drafting translations (always reviewed by a native speaker before being marked reviewed), reviewing code, writing tests and documentation. |

A change to this policy requires a public pull request explaining the data flow, and sign-off from the organizations running programs.
