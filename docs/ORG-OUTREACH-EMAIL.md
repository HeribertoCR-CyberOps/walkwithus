# Outreach email — draft for the founder to send personally

Send from your own mailbox, one organization at a time, after you have checked that the person is the right contact. Replace every [bracket]. Don't attach anything; link to the live page and the code.

---

**Subject:** A free tool for adults on corners at school times — yours to run, ours to support

Dear [Name],

I'm Heriberto Collazo, founder of Savvy Solution Pros, a cybersecurity firm in New York. After this week's events in the Bronx, my team built something we hope is useful to organizations like [Organization], and we're offering it free.

**Walk With Us** helps a community organization run a safe-passage program: trained adult volunteers standing at public corners during school arrival and dismissal, and two adults available to walk with a student who asks. It is the model Chicago has used since 2009; a peer-reviewed study of that program found violent crime about 14% lower along staffed routes, with no displacement to nearby blocks (Journal of Urban Economics, 2019).

**What it does**
- A public page for families, in English and Spanish, showing which corners are covered and when, with one-tap 911, NYPD Crime Stoppers and 988, and plain-language guidance on school safety transfers and bullying complaints.
- Volunteer sign-up. Your organization meets and approves every volunteer in person before their first shift.
- "Walk with me" requests. Families pick a public corner and a time; your coordinator calls back and assigns **two** approved adults. The system will not allow a walk with fewer than two.

**What it never does**
It never asks for a child's name and never tracks anyone's location. Family phone numbers are encrypted, seen only by your coordinators, and deleted automatically 72 hours after the walk. There are no ads, no trackers and no fees. The code is open source, so anyone can verify this.

**How the work divides**
- *Your organization runs the program:* choosing corners and hours, recruiting, meeting and briefing volunteers, coordinating walks, and the relationship with your schools.
- *We provide, free:* setup of your program page, a coordinator training session, security updates and monitoring, and help whenever something isn't working. We can have your page live within a day of a short call.

It is not an emergency service and it is not a reporting system; danger is always 911.

Would you have 30 minutes this week to see it? You can look first here: [live link] — and the code is public here: [repository link].
