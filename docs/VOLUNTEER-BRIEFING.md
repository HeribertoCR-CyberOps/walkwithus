# Volunteer briefing (15 minutes, in person, before the first shift)

Read it aloud. The volunteer confirms each point. The coordinator approves them in the dashboard only after this briefing.

## Your job
Be a calm, visible adult at your corner during the published hours. Your presence is the protection. You are a neighbor, not security and not police.

## The five rules
1. **Always at least two adults.** Never be alone with a student, never walk a student alone, never put a student in your car.
2. **Observe and report.** Never chase, confront, search, follow or detain anyone. Never try to break up a fight physically.
3. **Danger: call 911 first,** then your coordinator. Move yourself and students away from the danger.
4. **No photos, no posts.** Never photograph students or post about them, the program or incidents.
5. **Privacy.** Never share where a student lives, walks, or who asked for a walk.

## On shift
- Wear the organization's vest or badge.
- Check in on your phone when you arrive at your corner; check out when you leave. This records that an adult was there — it does not track you while you walk.
- If your partner hasn't arrived, tell the coordinator. A corner with one adult is shown as "needs a second adult."

## Walks
- The coordinator assigns walks to two adults and tells you the meeting corner and time. You will not receive the family's phone number.
- Meet at the corner, introduce yourselves with the reference code, walk the public route, stay together, part at the destination.

## If something happens
- Immediate danger → 911.
- A student says they are being threatened → listen, don't promise secrecy, tell the coordinator, who tells the family and school.
- Information about a crime → NYPD Crime Stoppers 1-800-577-TIPS (anonymous).

## Your link
Your check-in link is your key. Don't share it. Lost phone → tell your coordinator; they will issue a new one and the old one stops working immediately.
