# Name and endorsement policy

The **code** is free forever under AGPL-3.0-only (see `LICENSE` and `NOTICE`): anyone may use, study, change and share it at no cost, and nobody can make a closed, paid version of it.

The **name** "Walk With Us" and any statement that a program is endorsed by or connected with the project are different: families trust a program because of what the name promises. Savvy Solution Pros LLC, which started the project, sets these rules for using the name.

## You may call your program "Walk With Us" (or "powered by Walk With Us") only if it keeps every safety guarantee
1. It never collects a child's name, school, photo, route or location, and never tracks anyone's location.
2. Every walk has **two** approved adults (Rule of Two), and every volunteer is met in person and approved by the running organization before their first shift.
3. Family contact numbers are encrypted, seen only by coordinators, and deleted automatically after the walk (72 hours by default).
4. It shows no ads, embeds no trackers or third-party scripts, and uses no AI to make decisions about people.
5. 911 is one tap away on every page, and the program never presents itself as an emergency service.
6. It is offered free to families and volunteers.
7. It complies with the AGPL: users of a modified version can get its source code.

If your version changes any of these, use a different name. You may still say it is "based on Walk With Us software".

## You may not
- Say or imply that Savvy Solution Pros LLC, the City of New York, NYC Public Schools, the NYPD or any agency runs, approves or certifies your program unless that is true and in writing.
- Use the name for a paid product, for fundraising that does not go to running the program, or for political campaigning.

## Organizations running a program
The organization that runs a program (not the software maintainers) is responsible for its volunteers, corners, hours, and its relationship with schools and families. See `docs/FOR-ORGANIZATIONS.md`.

Questions or permission requests: heriberto@savvysolutionpros.com. This policy is not legal advice; it records the maintainers' conditions for using the project's name.
