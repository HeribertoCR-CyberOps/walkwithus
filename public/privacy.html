Privacy

We never collect

  • A child's name, photo, school record, or disciplinary information — about any child.
  • Anyone's live or background location. The app has no location permission and asks for none.
  • Free-text descriptions of people or incidents.
  • Advertising or analytics identifiers. There are no third-party scripts, fonts, maps or trackers.

We collect only

  • Walk requests: a public meeting corner, a 15-minute time slot, a language preference, and a callback phone number. The number is encrypted (AES-256-GCM) and readable only by the program's coordinators; each time one is viewed, that view is logged. The whole request is deleted automatically 72 hours after the walk time.
  • Volunteers: name, phone (both encrypted), preferred corners, and check-in/check-out times at public corners. Shift records are deleted after 72 hours; only anonymous totals (adult-minutes per corner per day) are kept for reporting.
  • Abuse protection: request counts keyed by a one-way hash that changes daily. Raw IP addresses are not stored.

Who runs this

Each program is run by the community organization named on its page, which is responsible for its volunteers and coordinators. The software is free and open source (AGPL-3.0); anyone can inspect exactly what it does.

This is not an emergency service. If someone is in danger, call 911.
